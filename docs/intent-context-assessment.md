The fresh real-Nemotron prefix assessment found inadequate request coverage at the preselected 0.95 threshold. The fitted contextual classifier selected 6 of 21 AI-reviewed ready fragments; the baseline selected none. Neither selected a premature-ready fragment at that threshold. Median CPU inference was 5.6 ms for the fitted model and 3.6 ms for the baseline. The gate remains disabled in production.

The missed requests include terse contextual followups and complete hypothetical challenges. A short assumption change can require a response when the preceding conversation establishes a problem to solve, while the same words can be background in a different conversation. Unspecified facts may call for clarification; they do not automatically make a request unfinished. An unfinished added clause still requires withholding an answer based on the earlier, incomplete version.

The [post-fit assessment](evidence/intent-context-assessment-v67.json) generated 20 new episode groups, 60 text cases, after freezing the model and 0.95 threshold. The new model's highest-probability label was correct on 58/60 cases, compared with 43/60 for the previous fitted classifier and 21/60 for the baseline. It distinguished all three variants correctly in 18/20 episodes, compared with 5/20 and 0/20. These are AI-reviewed synthetic contrast cases, not population accuracy or ASR evidence. The coding-agent quality review saw the contrast design and was not blind; the separate annotator saw each group variant in an isolated thread.

Confidence remained inadequate for early sending. At the frozen threshold, the new model selected only 1/20 ready requests with no premature selections; the previous fitted model selected 13/20 ready requests and one premature selection. Better label separation therefore does not establish a better sending policy. Median CPU inference was 5.6 ms. No threshold was selected from this assessment and no model was promoted. More realistic ASR/context assessment and development-based calibration work remain necessary.
