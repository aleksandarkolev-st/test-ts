## Space UI

`src/ui/Tabs.tsx` adapts the public primitives-tabs registry wrappers and indicator from https://www.spaceui.one/r/primitives-tabs.json. Sizes and Tailwind styling are replaced with the existing Meeting Copilot CSS. The source is published under MIT, as stated in https://www.spaceui.one/primitives/tabs.
